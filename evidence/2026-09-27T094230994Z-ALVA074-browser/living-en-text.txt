Welcome to the Living Design stage. Here, we will organize your everyday living needs, plan functional layouts, and explore tailored furniture arrangements and styles for your space.

To help us set the right design direction, please review the card below and select the option that best describes your primary goal. Once you make your choice, we will proceed with the next steps together.

Q01a · Example preview, not yet adopted
Which direction best reflects your main goal and current focus for this project?

Clarifying your primary objective will guide how deeply we plan the spatial zones, storage, and furniture arrangement.

Example premise:The confirmed room perimeter serves as the initial layout boundary.；Detailed lifestyle habits and storage demands will be refined progressively.

A · Set up a new home

Outcome if chosen:Establish complete room zones, traffic flow, and primary furniture placements for moving into a new residence.

Specific example:Map out everyday living and dining areas from scratch, then plan core storage capacity.

Trade-offs:Requires laying out full spatial zoning before focusing on localized details.

B · Renovate current layout

Outcome if chosen:Reconfigure existing room functions, partition layouts, and built-in fixtures for a comprehensive overhaul.

Specific example:Upgrade room circulation, rethink functional connections, and redesign built-in storage walls.

Trade-offs:Needs closer coordination with existing walls, plumbing, and structural boundaries.

C · Prioritize one room

Outcome if chosen:Direct all initial planning efforts toward perfecting function, layout, and furniture for one core room.

Specific example:Refine the confirmed living space first with dedicated reading, relaxing, and storage arrangements.

Trade-offs:Puts broader home areas on hold until this key room layout is settled.

D · Explore ideas freely

Outcome if chosen:Test different spatial arrangements and style directions without committing to an immediate build or move.

Specific example:Try various furniture configurations and aesthetic vibes to see what fits your lifestyle best.

Trade-offs:Focuses on conceptual possibilities rather than concrete execution milestones.
